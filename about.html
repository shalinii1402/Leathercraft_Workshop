Our Sacred Space

The Workshop

A Tradition of Excellence

Established in 2018, our workshop is located in the heart of the artisan district. It serves as both a production studio for our premium goods and a learning hub for aspiring leatherworkers.

We maintain a collection of vintage machines alongside traditional hand tools to bridge the gap between historical methods and modern efficiency.

History

Founded by master artisan David Thorne after 20 years in the Italian leather industry.
